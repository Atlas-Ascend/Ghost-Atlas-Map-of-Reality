## Autonomous preparation law

Later campaign packets may prepare in parallel, but must not falsely close ahead of dependencies.

Examples:

- commercial offer design may proceed before the physical baseline closes;
- hackathon products may build independently;
- public surfaces may be prepared;
- Euna experiments may continue.

But none of these substitute for physical/runtime proof.

## Human-only gates

Preserve explicit human authority for:

- privileged physical-machine actions where required;
- credentials and secret entry;
- destructive/irreversible actions;
- protected production promotion;
- contractual/financial commitments;
- legal entity formation;
- external customer commitment;
- physical playtesting/device ceremony where necessary.

## Campaign Definition of Done

The campaign is DONE when there is evidence for the following statement:

> Ghost Atlas is a physically anchored, temporally consistent, governed, resident software organism that maintains a canonical Map of Reality; converts bounded human intent into capability-routed work; executes under explicit authority across available compute; independently verifies and remembers outcomes; detects and recovers from failure; distinguishes simulation from live state; operates from a reproducible sealed production baseline; and returns externally observed consequences into its map for the next reasoning cycle.

At that point the primary question changes from **“Can the machine become real?”** to **“What verified capabilities should this machine operate, distribute, sell, study, and improve next?”**

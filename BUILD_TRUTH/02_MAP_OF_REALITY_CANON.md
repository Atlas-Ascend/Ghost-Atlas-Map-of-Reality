# 02 — Map of Reality Canon

## Canonical statement

**Universal Atlas is the Map of Reality. Vishvarupa is the organism that lives and acts within that map.**

The Map of Reality is not a static diagram. It is a continuously versioned, evidence-backed model of entities, relationships, current state, historical state, observer state, authority, capability, work, execution, embodiment, proof, feedback and candidate futures.

## Map dimensions

The canonical reality record is multidimensional. At minimum it includes:

### Ontological dimension
What classes of things exist and which identity rules apply.

### Relational dimension
How entities are connected: owner, dependency, producer, consumer, authority, executor, parent, child, fork, merge, ancestor, descendant, interface, failure route, proof route.

### Temporal dimension
What was true at time `T`, what changed, and what remains unresolved.

### Epistemic dimension
How a claim is known: declared, observed, inferred, simulated, historical, verified, externally confirmed.

### Cognitive dimension
Which observer/state model is active or relevant and with what confidence.

### Authority dimension
Who may decide, mutate, promote, publish, spend, delete, expose, or restore.

### Capability dimension
What a role, agent, model, node, organ or service can actually do, under what limits.

### Operational dimension
Mission, Packet, queue, lease, executor, retries, completion state and handoffs.

### Physical dimension
Machine, device, CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, OS, process, service, network, power class, model residency and health.

### Proof dimension
Claim, evidence, test, receipt, hash, verifier, verdict and acceptance boundary.

### Simulation dimension
Possible worlds, candidates, experiments, predictions and Euna-only state.

### External dimension
Customer, competition, deployment, market, institution, legal/company and other outside-world interactions.

## Identity invariance

The same canonical entity may have multiple names, repositories, representations, models, interfaces or historical bodies. The Map must preserve identity relationships explicitly rather than flattening aliases.

Allowed lineage relations include:

`SAME_AS`, `ALIAS_OF`, `ANCESTOR_OF`, `DESCENDANT_OF`, `FORK_OF`, `MERGED_INTO`, `SUPERSEDES`, `REIMPLEMENTATION_OF`, `CONCEPTUAL_PARENT_OF`, `RUNTIME_OF`, `REPRESENTATION_OF`.

## World principle

Ghost Atlantia, dashboards, visualizations, graphs, Unreal scenes, mobile interfaces and public websites are **renderers over canonical state**, not authorities over canonical state.

No renderer may create truth merely by displaying it.

## Return law

Every externally consequential action creates a potential map delta. The organism is incomplete if it can act but cannot integrate the result.

Canonical terminal loop:

`MODEL → AUTHORIZE → ACT → OBSERVE → PROVE → REMEMBER → UPDATE MAP → REASON AGAIN`

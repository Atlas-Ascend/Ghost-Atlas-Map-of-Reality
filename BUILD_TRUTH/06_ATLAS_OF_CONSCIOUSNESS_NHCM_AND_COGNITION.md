# 06 — Atlas of Consciousness, NHCM, and Cognition

## Canonical role

The **Atlas of Consciousness** maps observer-state space. It is the internal complement to Universal Atlas.

- Universal Atlas asks: **What exists, how is it related, and what is its state?**
- Atlas of Consciousness asks: **From what observer/state configuration is reality being interpreted, and what cognitive capacities are active?**

The two maps meet inside Atlas Mind / Vishvarupa.

## Authority boundary

Atlas of Consciousness and NHCM may model, classify and visualize reported or system-observed states. They do not declare human spiritual rank, override self-determination, diagnose medical conditions, or silently convert interpretation into authority.

The technical principle is **observer modeling with epistemic humility**.

## ARCADIA

ARCADIA is the governing cognitive architecture: the structural rules that keep multiple cognitive systems from collapsing into one unconstrained agent.

Responsibilities:

- define cognitive module boundaries;
- prevent memory, simulation, authority and execution from being conflated;
- preserve uncertainty;
- expose module provenance;
- support multiple candidate interpretations;
- enforce handoff to JANUS before consequential action.

## OmniMind / Mothership

OmniMind and Mothership are high-level cognitive/control-plane surfaces over the organism. They may unify context, status and interaction across organs, but they remain consumers and orchestrators of authoritative state rather than replacement sources of truth.

## Rainbow Council / plural cognition

Where multiple models, agents or perspectives are consulted, the system should preserve:

- individual proposals;
- confidence;
- evidence;
- dissent;
- uncertainty;
- final authority decision;
- later consequence.

Consensus alone is not proof.

## Cognitive safety invariants

1. Interpretation ≠ authority.
2. Confidence ≠ evidence.
3. Simulation ≠ observation.
4. Agent plurality ≠ truth by majority.
5. Human root authority remains explicit.
6. Uncertainty is stored rather than erased.
7. Cognitive state may influence routing but cannot silently expand permissions.
8. Model identity is substitutable beneath role/authority contracts.

## Proof gates

The cognition layer is qualified when it can:

1. normalize a founder request without changing its intent;
2. produce at least two candidate interpretations with provenance;
3. carry uncertainty into JANUS decisioning;
4. bind current Atlas state + Thoth history + NHCM state without conflating their truth classes;
5. deny a simulated/Euna candidate direct production authority;
6. preserve minority dissent through decision and outcome;
7. replace one underlying model without changing the role/authority contract;
8. return the final verified outcome to the updated cognitive context.

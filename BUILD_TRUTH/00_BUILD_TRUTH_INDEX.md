1. **Generation is not permission.** Euna or any generative system may produce candidates; JANUS/authority and SECA/proof govern promotion.
2. **Repository truth is not physical truth.** A commit or green CI result cannot prove physical EDEN state without physical evidence.
3. **Designed ≠ built ≠ running ≠ verified ≠ resident ≠ production.** Every lifecycle transition requires its own evidence.
4. **No competing control planes.** Agent populations, offices, products, competitions, and research bodies route through the canonical authority/work/proof spine.
5. **No silent truth overwrite.** Historical snapshots remain historically true; SESHAT records deltas rather than replacing prior states.
6. **Production is immutable by default.** Development evolves; promoted production consumes versioned, checksummed artifacts.
7. **Forward repair and backward restoration are separate.** Prometheus proposes; MetaForge implements; OSIRIS restores; SECA judges; JANUS authorizes.
8. **Every mutation has an authority envelope and a proof contract.**
9. **Every organ has owner, inputs, outputs, permissions, handoffs, health signal, failure route, and evidence class.**
10. **The map closes the loop.** External effects return as evidence into Universal Atlas/SESHAT/Thoth.

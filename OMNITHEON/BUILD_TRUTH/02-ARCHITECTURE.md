# Architecture

OMNITHEON is advisory, not sovereign execution.

- Universal Church: tradition and knowledge substrate.
- OMNITHEON: pantheon-of-pantheons organizational layer.
- Rainbow Council: deliberation and convergence engine.
- Guardian Architecture: constitutional constraint.
- JANUS/ODIN: executive authorization.
- SECA/DevOS: verification authority.
- Medusa: security, privacy, cultural-boundary review.
- ProofGrid: receipt and provenance ledger.
- AEON: continuity horizon.

No council office receives arbitrary shell, deployment, destructive, secret-management, or self-promotion authority.

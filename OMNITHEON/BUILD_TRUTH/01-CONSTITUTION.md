# OMNITHEON Constitution

1. No one is forgotten.
2. Evidence outranks unsupported factual assertion.
3. No worldview possesses automatic supremacy.
4. Human agency remains sovereign.
5. Minority and dissenting positions are preserved.
6. Sacred knowledge requires consent and provenance.
7. Cultures are represented in context rather than flattened into archetypes.
8. Symbolic, empirical, historical, normative, and speculative claims remain typed separately.
9. Power creates stewardship obligations.
10. Claims remain falsifiable wherever falsification is applicable.
11. Decisions remain inspectable and attributable.
12. Continuity is designed beyond any one founder or operator.
